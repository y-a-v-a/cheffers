The language

What is Chef?

Chef is an esoteric programming language created by David Morgan-Mar in 2002. Programs in Chef look like cooking recipes — ingredients represent variables, mixing bowls are stacks, and cooking instructions translate to program operations.

When executed, your recipe doesn't just cook a dish — it computes!

  • Ingredient→variable
  • Mixing bowl→stack
  • Baking dish→output
  • Refrigerator→input
  • Sous-chef→function
